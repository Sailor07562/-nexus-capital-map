# NEXUS FOUNDATION — CANON

Created: 2026-09-22

Status: review candidate; not authoritative until merged through repository review.

## Prime directive

Governance above all.

Nexus exists to turn intent into controlled action.

We decide what we need. The engine does not define its own mission.

Governance defines the lane before any engine operates inside it.

## Future-engine rule

No engine without a declared purpose.

No purpose without boundaries.

No boundary without receipts.

No action without authority.

An engine is not a script, workflow, dashboard, or vibe. An engine is a governed capability with an inspectable authority surface, explicit stop conditions, and Command Tower visibility.

Capabilities may exist before they are engines. Naming a capability does not promote it.

## Operating doctrine

Engines may assist.

PostgreSQL records.

Governance decides.

The human authorizes.

## Required questions for every future engine

1. What need does this engine serve?
2. What problem is it allowed to solve?
3. What evidence must it use?
4. What can it observe, calculate, recommend, or execute?
5. What is forbidden no matter what?
6. Where does it log receipts?
7. What promotes it from sandbox to paper to production?
8. Where does it stop and ask for human authority?

## Engine admission standard

Before promotion, every engine must have:

- declared purpose;
- explicit boundaries;
- PostgreSQL or Nexus Governance authority surface;
- readback views or receipts;
- regression or compliance checks;
- Command Tower visibility;
- human authority stop.

## Current engine canon

- Scraper / Intake Engine — governed capability.
- Decision Engine — formal live engine.
- Investing Engine — formal live orchestration engine.
- Capital Deployment Engine — distributed control plane.
- Research Radar Engine — review-only capability.
- Consumer Intelligence / Consumer Review Engine — review-only capability.
- Governance Engine — candidate until authority mapping and compliance tests are explicit.
- Infrastructure Context Engine — candidate until refreshed and re-validated.

## Capital boundary

No engine receives live capital authority by default.

Paper execution may be armed only inside the approved policy gate.

Live trading, transfers, margin, short sales, options, and crypto remain forbidden unless a separate future governance record explicitly authorizes them.

## Current Foundation components

- PostgreSQL is the semantic authority.
- n8n is the executor.
- Command Tower is the cockpit.
- Receipts are memory.
- Governance is the guardrail.
- Paper trading is the proving ground.
- The human remains the authority.
